Where are pinned screenshots stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device. Pinned screenshots are automatically deleted when the pin expires, unless you turn on “Keep screenshots after pin ends” in Settings. Screenshots are never uploaded.

Is anything uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing — including smart cropping — happens on-device. PinIsland has no server, no analytics, and no ad SDKs.

How do I stop an island from showing?

Open the pinned screenshot in the app and tap the delete button. The island also disappears automatically when its duration ends (15 minutes to 4 hours, configurable in Settings).

Why is the island preview hidden on the Lock Screen?

That is the privacy option at work. Go to Settings → Live Activity Display and choose “Show preview” to display it on the Lock Screen, or “Hide preview” to keep it private.

How do I install the Shortcut?

Go to Settings → Shortcuts and tap Install. The link opens Apple’s Shortcuts app; follow the prompts to add it. You can remove the shortcut at any time in the Shortcuts app.
