Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

PinIsland is built with privacy first. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app — in short: everything stays on your device.

Data We Collect

PinIsland does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There is no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

How Screenshots Are Handled

Screenshots you pin are processed entirely on your device, including the smart cropping model. No image is ever uploaded to a server or shared with any third party. Pinned screenshots are automatically deleted when the pin ends, unless you turn on “Keep screenshots after pin ends” in Settings — even then they remain only in the app’s private local storage and are never transmitted.

Local Settings & Live Activity

Your preferences are saved locally on the device and shared only with the app’s own Live Activity widget, so the island behaves consistently. The widget only ever accesses the currently pinned screenshot. On the Lock Screen, you can choose “Hide preview” in Settings → Live Activity Display for extra privacy.

Shortcuts & System Integration

PinIsland can optionally install a Siri Shortcut for faster pinning. The installation link simply opens Apple’s Shortcuts app; no data is sent or received in the process. You can remove the shortcut at any time in the Shortcuts app.

Third-Party Services

PinIsland uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, or ad networks. The on-device smart crop model was trained using the FastViT-T8 architecture by Apple; see Third-Party Licenses for details.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and on the app’s official website.
